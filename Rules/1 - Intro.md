# DEAD RECKONING: ADMIRAL'S LOG

A solo journalling RPG of survival, command and sacrifice.

---

You are the Admiral of a rag-tag fleet of dozens of surviving starships fleeing a devastating attack on their colony. The enemy fleet is in pursuit and could jump in and attack at any time. The civilian ships are unarmed and rely on your carrier and its fighters to defend them.

Resources are limited and you are vastly outnumbered. Your only hope is to coordinate the fleet to perform FTL jumps as quickly as possible. There are legends of a distant homeworld that could offer safety or perhaps you know of somewhere else.

Your own crewmembers

The acting President of our colony

---
## Starting a campaign

- Name your carrier ship by rolling on the ship name table or choosing one yourself.
- Describe your carrier and note any interesting features.
- Create some character/ship/faction cards.

## Create a character

Take a blank index card and write the following on it, rolling on tables or simply writing what makes sense to you.
- Character name - what people call them.
- Character role - the position they hold in their ship or faction.
- Character trait - a defining aspect of their character.
- Ship name - the name of the ship that they're on.
- Ship role - the purpose that the ship serves.
- Ship detail - an unusual aspect of the ship.
- Faction name - the group that they belong to or represent.
- (Faction type and disposition???)

## Gameplay loop
1. Draw a card. This character has either approached you or you have become aware of them somehow. They have an issue that requires your attention.
2. 
	- If the card doesn't already hold an issue, create one and write it on.
	- If the card already contains an issue, this issue has returned but is more urgent. Raise its level by 1 to maximum of 3.
