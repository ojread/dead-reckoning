Roll on the top table to determine the issue type and need. Then go to the section for that issue type below and roll on the situation, focus and twist columns.

| d6  | Issue type                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Resource - scarcity, allocation, hoarding, theft. The fleet never has enough of anything.        |
| 2   | Authority - who has the right to decide? And what happens when that is challenged?               |
| 3   | Loyalty - allegiances, betrayal, suspicion. The line between trust and betrayal is rarely clear. |
| 4   | Belief - ideology, faith, identity, morality. The things that people will not compromise on.     |
| 5   | Information - knowledge, secrets, lies. What is known and by whom?                               |
| 6   | External - hostile forces attack the fleet.                                                      |
